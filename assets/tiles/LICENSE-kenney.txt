Tile and sprite sources for assets/tiles/tileset.png and assets/tiles/sprites.png
(built by tool/mapgen/make_tiles.py).

- tileset.png: grass, tree, dirt path, court from Kenney "Tiny Town" 1.1;
  sand, parking and road are recoloured Tiny Town tiles; water and roof are our own drawings.
- sprites.png: player (recoloured to blue) and chests from Kenney "Tiny Dungeon" 1.0;
  sparkle frames are our own drawing.
- Our own drawings are released under CC0 1.0 as well.
